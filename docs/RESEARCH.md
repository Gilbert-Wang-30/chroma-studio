## 1. Executive summary: the highest-value changes, ranked by expected gain per unit of effort

1. **Shading-invariant linkage distance in `grouping.cluster_colors`** (the `blend` rule: CIEDE2000 on lightness-normalised chromaticity, parametric kC = 2 for chromatic pairs, chroma-gated lightness weight 0.15, gate 12→30, same threshold 10). FM1. Measured paint-locked main share: RX-78 0.531→1.000, Sazabi 0.941→0.993, red car 0.858→0.961; Ducati and Exia unchanged; zero wrong merges on five of six jobs, two on the BMW. About one day. Source: `scratch/experiments/grouping/README.md` (trials 3-6, Numbers table). **adopt**
2. **OKLab chroma plane with a CIELAB-like toe on OK lightness in `recolor/engine.py`** (`oklab_toe`). FM6, FM5. Measured: BMW navy hue drift worst bin 23.0°→7.1°, mean 4.8°→2.1° (the shipping navy reads violet, the OKLab one blue); Ducati navy mean 0.7°→0.4° but the top 1.1 % of the paint (highlights) drifts −21° cyan-ward instead of −9°; black-repaint darkness preserved; halo harness unchanged (11484→11473 px). One to two days (`scratch/experiments/oklab/engine_oklab.py` is a finished port). Source: `scratch/experiments/oklab/README.md`. Link: https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/. **adopt**
3. **FM11 step inside the engine: gated hue rotation of old-paint-hued pixels outside the repainted groups**, using the engine's own θ and chroma scale min(1, C_T/C_A). Measured as a hard-mask control: reflection survival 1.00→0.00 on both bikes, PSNR vs the engine render outside the paint 37.2 dB (Ducati) / 26.5 dB (BMW), ΔE 0 inside the paint. Needs a soft gate (chroma, hue distance, distance to the repainted groups, not-in-locked-group) so the Ducati's gold wheels (hue 68°, 30° from the red) are not caught. One to two days. Source: `scratch/experiments/rerender/README.md` trial 5. **adopt (with gate)**
4. **Decal outlier pre-split before grouping** (`scratch/experiments/grouping/decal_split.py`: pixels > 25 ΔE from the region median and > 30 lower in chroma, blobs ≥ 40 px, no dilation). FM1, FM2. Isolates the "DUCATI" tank decal (1042 px) and fairing decal (5048 px) as their own regions; costs bleed 17→100 px on the Ducati because the decal rim becomes an unpainted neighbour, so land it with item 5. One day. **adopt (paired with 5)**
5. **Colour-guided label refinement.** FM2, FM10. `filters.refine_labels_with_guide` collapses an RGB guide to Rec.709 luminance (`filters.py`), while the engine already has the full-colour solver `filters.guided_filter_color` for coverage. Use the colour solver in `hierarchy._refine_edges` (radius 2) and `pipeline._render_full` (radius 4). Unmeasured; the harness (halo/bleed/jaggy) is the metric. One day. **experiment (cheap)**
6. **Marigold-IID shading colour grafted onto Careaga's layers** (`mg_hyb`: keep Careaga albedo and shading luminance, take the shading chromaticity from Marigold-IID lighting). FM3. Measured: Ducati navy hue drift 0.7°/9.4° → 0.4°/3.4° (mean / worst bin), BMW 4.8°/23.0° → 2.1°/20.9°; sharpness unchanged; +0.9 s per job, 6.0 GB peak, one 2.6 GB CreativeML Open RAIL++-M checkpoint plus `diffusers` in the venv. About a week including the venv work. Source: `scratch/experiments/intrinsic/README.md`. **experiment → adopt if the four other jobs agree**
7. **A material advisory channel instead of auto-locks.** FM4, FM8. Per-region cue from data already on disk (positive-residual energy fraction and shading chroma per region), then a local zero-shot classifier on region crops (CLIP/SigLIP, or Grounding DINO boxes turned into SAM 2 masks), written to a new `Region.material_hint` and surfaced as a badge and a "suggest unmapped" default in `mapping.suggest_mapping`. Never a silent lock: on the Ducati and BMW jobs chrome, tyres and glass share colour groups with paint (section 2, FM4). About a week. Sources: GaussianProperty, GDNet-B and Grounding DINO cards (fit-check corrections). **experiment**
8. **Boundary refinement for thin parts**: HQ-SAM 2 (Apache-2.0, checkpoints), SAMRefiner (MIT, training-free, refines our own coarse masks), CascadePSP (MIT, `pip install segmentation-refinement`, also usable at export resolution). FM2, FM10. About a week for a three-way comparison on hand-labelled thin-part masks plus the harness. **experiment**
9. **Full-resolution export path**: colour guide for both label snapping and layer upsampling, joint-bilateral / bilateral-guided upsampling as the reference families (Kopf 2007; Chen 2016, Apache-2.0), tiled full-res Careaga above 12 MP. FM10. About a week, with a new full-res edge test (section 4). **experiment**
10. **Ground truth and perceptual metrics** (FM9): synthetic recolour pairs from re-rendered scenes (Stanford-ORB, OpenRooms, Hypersim), the real paired set ColourfulSet to inspect, LPIPS/DISTS/FLIP in `scripts/` only (licences), a boundary-offset test and a wrong-lock/correct-lock test for FM4. One to two weeks. **experiment**
11. **Big bet: a per-pixel roughness/metallic cue for a dielectric-vs-metallic specular model** (FM4, FM5). The RGB→X channels are already cached and produce "plausible roughness/metallic" at 8.5 s per photo and 4.8 GB (rerender README trial 1), but the weights are under the Adobe Research licence and no gain is measured yet. **experiment (big bet)**
12. **Big bet: semantic palette and mapping** (FM7, FM8): harmony re-ranking from the O'Donovan 2011 compatibility data and an optional, opt-in LLM palette suggester (the `anthropic` SDK is installed in the venv, `openai` is not); the offline fallback of `generate_palette` must stay. **experiment (big bet)**

Not recommended after measurement: diffusion re-rendering for reflections (RGB↔X: PSNR 5-7 dB far from the object, deletes reflections rather than recolouring them), harmonization networks (IntrinsicHarmony, PCT-Net: their masks cannot reach the pixels FM11 is about), DINOv2 / DINO-ViT features for grouping (fragment every paint by part), SAV (no weights, toolchain cannot run on the 5090).

With `feather_px` 0 (edge-snapped hard coverage): halo 22077, bleed 3, worst jaggy 2.27. The outward-only ramp buys a halving of the halo for a few hundred pixels of bleed: that trade is the signature of label boundaries sitting inside the true edge (FM2).

**FM1 Grouping.** `grouping.group_regions` → `_cluster_regions` → `cluster_colors`: area-weighted centroid-linkage agglomeration on each region's median albedo in CIELAB, plain CIEDE2000, threshold 10. The albedo is not shading-free: inside the paint it explains 37 % (Ducati) and 51 % (BMW) of the luminance variance (intrinsic README, `careaga` rows), so a lit panel and a shadowed one of the same paint exceed ΔE 10 and become separate groups: RX-78 red is three groups (main share 0.531), the BMW's 12 groups contain six amber-hue paint groups (main share 0.911), the red car's paint 0.858 (grouping README trial 1 and Numbers table; the brief's "seven yellow groups" is that same fragmentation). The washed-out cases are different: the red car's bonnet reflecting the ceiling has chroma 34 vs 78 at the same lightness, the BMW's tail underside 41 vs 79, which no multiplicative model explains (trial 4). The decal case is in `hierarchy._split_bimodal`: it needs the smaller mode to hold ≥ `SPLIT_MIN_FRACTION` 0.08 of the region and both parts ≥ `min_px`, so a 1042 px decal inside a tank never splits. The engine then repaints each fragment with its own lightness anchor (`Renderer._group_params`: L' = T_L + slope·(L − A_L)), which is what puts a seam across the RX-78's chest (grouping README, "What the sheets show").

**FM2 Segmentation.** `SamMasker` presets (32/40/64 points per side, 0/1/2 crop layers, m2m on for balanced and max; 0.7 / ~5 / ~19 s per 1536 px image with the GPU idle, 4.5-7.8 GB), then `hierarchy.build_regions`: largest-first painting with duplicate rejection (overlap 0.85, ΔE 4), bimodal split (ΔE 14, valley 0.5), SLIC fill (300-600 px per superpixel, merge below ΔE 8), guided-filter edge snap with `REFINE_RADIUS` 2, speck removal. The snap goes through `filters.refine_labels_with_guide`, which reduces a colour guide to luminance, so an edge between two surfaces of equal brightness and different hue (red panel on grey, blue vent in a black gap) is invisible to it; the engine's `_coverage` had to grow its own colour solver (`guided_filter_color`) for exactly that reason. Labels are hard `int32`; anti-aliased pixels take one side. Thin parts below the preset's `min_area_frac` (0.0006 of the image at balanced, i.e. about 940 px at 1536×1024) are never regions of their own.

**FM3 Intrinsic leaks.** Careaga & Aksoy v2.1 at the 1536 px working size (`careaga.decompose_careaga`: reflect-pad to a multiple of 32, crop back, residual recomputed so `lin = albedo·shading + residual` holds in float32; 0.49 s per job in `job.json`, 4.8 GB). Measured leak inside the paint (intrinsic README): Ducati shading chroma median 10.3 / p90 33.6, BMW 4.6 / 9.4; residual energy 0.14 / 0.19 of the paint's energy; the engine docstring's own numbers are 37 % redder light under the RX-78 shield and five times redder than blue in the Ducati tank shadows. The engine fights this in three places: `_light_reference` (median chromaticity of the shading over well-lit, low-chroma albedo pixels, a white-balance estimate), `_retint_shading` (projects the light's tint beyond that illuminant onto the old paint's chroma direction and rotates it to the new paint) and `_adjusted_residual` (splits the positive residual at its achromatic floor). Two limits are measured: the projection direction is the paint's, while the actual leak is 17-22° warmer than the paint (oklab README trial 3), and the BMW's +23° highlight hue drift lives in the albedo, so no shading-side work removes it (intrinsic README: `mg_hyb` fixes the Ducati and not the BMW). The heuristic fallback (`intrinsic/heuristic.py`) is a gradient-domain Retinex with a Poisson solve and a grey shading.

**FM4 No material awareness.** Nothing in `Region` or `ColorGroup` describes material; the only mechanism is `ColorGroup.locked` (UI toggle in `web/js/panels/groups.js`, `PATCH /api/jobs/{id}/groups/{gid}`), honoured by `suggest_mapping(keep_locked=True)` and engine rule 7. Because grouping is albedo-only, non-paint parts land wherever their colour lands. Inspected on the jobs on disk (GaussianProperty fit-check): Ducati rear wheel and tyre sit inside the background group g0, the front wheel in "Khaki" g2 with a backdrop corner, "Silver" g4 = a 49.6 k px floor piece + headlight glass + fork + brake calliper; BMW "Black" g1 = 31 regions mixing tyres, engine, seat, mirrors and the black painted fairing lowers. Metallic and pearlescent paints are rendered as diffuse albedo plus a neutral glint like any other paint.

**FM5 Highlights and speculars.** `_Level.spec_weight` calls a pixel a glint by one global rule: the neutral floor of the positive residual divided by its 0.99 quantile over the whole image. `_adjusted_residual` rebuilds the coloured excess as a multiple of the repainted product, keeps the neutral floor where `spec` is high and fades it with the repaint where it is low, scales the negative (clipping) residual by the new-to-old product ratio, and `residual_tint` optionally pulls what survives toward the target. The colour of a sensor-clipped highlight is unknown, so on a new colour it is re-rendered from whatever the decomposition guessed: on a navy target the highest lightness bins drift +23° (BMW) and −9.4° (Ducati) in the CIELAB engine (oklab README, hue table). The oklab experiment shows part of that is colour space (FM6) and part is the bounce retint. There is no dielectric/metallic distinction and no gloss estimate.

**FM6 Colour math.** All repaint arithmetic is CIELAB (`srgb_to_lab_t`, `_recolored_albedo`, `_retint_shading`). Gamut handling is not a clip: `lab_to_linear_gamut_t` binary-searches the chroma scale at fixed L and hue (six halvings), which is the CSS Color 4 strategy in a different space; the problem is the space. CIELAB's blue hue line bends toward purple as lightness rises, so the shipping BMW navy reads violet (worst bin +23°) and the same render held at constant OK hue reads blue (oklab README, final table and `compare_yellow_navy_crop.jpg`). Pastel targets have a second problem no fixed-L rule fixes: the target's chroma does not exist above its own lightness, so 36-39 % of a pastel repaint sits on the gamut boundary in every mode (trial 5).

**FM7 Palette.** `palette.generate_palette` precedence: ≥ 2 explicit colour words (`colornames.parse_color_words`) → one of 57 curated themes (`themes.match_theme`) → Wikimedia Commons / ddgs thumbnails, chroma-weighted k-means with k = n + 4, merge within ΔE 9, drop < 2 % (`extract.extract_palette`) → `FALLBACK_COLORS`. It never fails offline. It has no notion of what "racing livery" means beyond a theme row or an image search, and no harmony model.

**FM8 Mapping.** `mapping._cost_matrix`: balanced = 0.45·|area-rank difference| + 0.45·|ΔL|/100 + 0.10·hue distance/180, neutral-to-chromatic penalty 0.30, Hungarian assignment, nearest-Lab fill when groups outnumber colours. Locked and background groups are the only exclusions; nothing knows a wheel from a body panel.

**FM9 Evaluation.** Five proxies on six photos (`halo_eval.py`: halo, bleed, jaggy, interior ΔE, identity) plus the experiment metrics in `scratch/experiments/common/metrics.py` (OKLab hue drift per lightness bin, fragmentation, reflection survival, leak). No ground-truth pairs, no perceptual metric, no user study; several proxies saturate (identity is always 0, reflection survival is 1.00 by construction for anything that only edits inside the paint).

**FM10 Full-resolution export.** `pipeline._render_full`: the group map is nearest-upsampled (`filters.upsample_labels`) and snapped with the luminance-guided `refine_labels_with_guide` at radius 4 when H·W·min(G,32) ≤ 320 M elements; the intrinsic model is re-run on the original when it is ≤ 12 MP and the VRAM estimate (1.0 + 1.5 GB/MP) fits, otherwise `filters.guided_filter` (luminance guide, radius 8, eps 1e-3) upsamples the 1536 px albedo and shading and the residual is recomputed. Above 12 MP the texture inside a part is therefore 1536 px content smoothed along luminance edges, chroma edges are not followed, and the boundary geometry is the working-resolution label map's.

**FM11 Global consistency.** Engine rule 7 leaves every unmapped pixel bit-exact, so reflections of the repainted part keep the old colour: 2720 old-hue pixels ≥ 20 px outside the Ducati paint survive at 100 % (rerender README). On the BMW the 16837 such pixels are mostly the un-grouped nose and upper fairing, i.e. an FM1 miss that inflates the FM11 number. Bounce onto neighbours is only recoloured inside the coverage field `m` (`_retint_shading` weights by `m`), never on the adjacent unpainted part.

## 3. Methods by failure mode

### FM1 Grouping

- **adopt: shading-invariant `blend` distance** (in-house, `scratch/experiments/grouping/grouplib.py`). Drop-in replacement for the linkage distance in `grouping.cluster_colors`; greys keep the shipped CIEDE2000 exactly, chromatic pairs use the normalised-chromaticity distance with kC = 2 and lightness weight 0.15. Gains and limits: RX-78 0.531→1.000, Sazabi 0.941→0.993, red car 0.858→0.961, group counts fall (Ducati 12→11, RX-78 13→9); two wrong merges on the BMW that a colour rule cannot avoid (mirror stalk r44, black plastic whose albedo carries the paint's interreflection; shadowed panel r50 merging with the gold fork r41 at d 6.0 before the paint at 7.0). Merging widens each group's lightness span, which exposes the CIELAB hue bend on bright pieces (RX-78 mean drift 1.6°→7.0° while the worst bin improves 56°→25°), so land it with the OKLab work. Classical basis: the illumination-invariant colour spaces of Finlayson, Drew & Lu (ECCV 2004 / PAMI 2006) and Gevers & Smeulders' c1c2c3 (Pattern Recognition 1999) — closed-form, no learning; listed in the gap bibliography, not deep-read.
- **adopt (with item 5): decal outlier split** (`decal_split.py`, grouping README item 9). Pixel-level carve-out of low-chroma, far-from-median blobs inside large saturated regions before grouping; the Ducati decals become their own regions and group with the silver parts. Regression: bleed 17→100 px, halo 1884→2088 on the Ducati, from the newly exposed decal rim. Needs the colour-guided edge snap (FM2) to keep the rim with the right side.
- **reject: DINOv2** (Oquab et al. 2023, arXiv 2304.07193; Apache-2.0 code and ViT-S/B/L/g weights). The exact blend-into-linkage mechanism was built and swept (grouping README trials 7-8): w ≥ 0.2 fragments every paint into its parts (yellow 0.737 and exia 0.505 at w 0.2, ducati 0.521 at w 0.3), w = 0.1 changes nothing on the paints and adds groups (Ducati 15→21 from the `norm:10` base). The fit-check refuted the follow-up "merge-veto" idea with the cached features: the wrong merges sit at DINO distance 0.32 (r44 to the paint centroid) while the correct merges on RX-78/Sazabi/red car sit at 0.58-0.94, so no threshold works. Cost is not the problem (0.6 s load, 21 ms at 1540×1022, 0.41-0.51 GB, runs on torch 2.14+cu130 without xFormers). The paper itself calls part correspondence "an emerging property" the model was not trained for (Section 10).
- **reject: Deep ViT Features as Dense Visual Descriptors** (Amir et al. 2022, arXiv 2112.05814; MIT). Same mechanism, older backbone. Fact-check corrections: SPair-71k PCK@0.1 is 56.48 % at layer 9 and 53.45 % at layer 11 (61.43 % is the supervised CATs baseline). Fit-check: the extractor is built for ~224 px inputs; at 1536 px with stride 8 the original attention would need roughly 14 GB (ViT-S/8) to 29 GB (ViT-B/8) in fp16, and `pydensecrf` has no installable distribution for this venv. The optional cheap falsification (keys-facet distances on the eight decisive BMW pairs in `result.json`) needs no sweep.
- **experiment: Materialistic** (Sharma et al., SIGGRAPH 2023, arXiv 2305.13291; code and Google-Drive weights at `prafull7/materialistic`, no licence file, Python 3.9 / pytorch-lightning). It learns "same material under different lighting and pose" from one click, which is the invariance FM1 needs and DINO lacks. Use as a pairwise merge cue between regions (query pixel in region A, response averaged over region B) in a scratch sweep like the DINO one; licence must be clarified before anything leaves `scratch/`.
- **reference-only: palette-based decomposition and soft colour segmentation** (Tan et al. 2018 RGBXY, MIT; Aksoy et al. 2017 unmixing; Fast Soft Color Segmentation, Akimoto et al. CVPR 2020, official MIT code with a pretrained 7-layer model; Chang et al. 2015; Tan et al. 2016). They decompose the photo, not the albedo, into per-pixel soft layers along a small palette; shading ends up inside the layers, so they do not solve FM1, but a soft per-pixel colour layer is a possible source of mixed-pixel coverage for FM2 (see there).

### FM2 Segmentation and boundaries

- **experiment (cheap): colour guide in `refine_labels_with_guide`** (in-house). `filters.guided_filter_color` already solves the 3×3 covariance system per pixel; a chunked label version of it replaces the luminance path in `hierarchy._refine_edges` and `pipeline._render_full`. Metric: halo/bleed/jaggy on the six jobs.
- **experiment: HQ-SAM / HQ-SAM 2** (Ke et al., NeurIPS 2023, arXiv 2306.01567; `SysCV/sam-hq`, Apache-2.0; HQ-SAM 2 beta checkpoints released 2024-11-17). A high-quality output token fused with early and late ViT features fixes broken thin structures. Change: `sam_masks.py` loads the HQ variant behind the same `generate` interface; everything downstream is unchanged. Unverified for our domain; measure thin-part recall on hand-labelled masks plus the harness.
- **experiment: SAMRefiner** (Lin et al., ICLR 2025, arXiv 2502.06756; MIT; training-free, built on SAM/HQ-SAM). Refines a coarse mask by deriving prompts from it; our labels are exactly such coarse masks, so it can run per region after `build_regions` without touching SAM's proposal stage.
- **experiment: CascadePSP** (Cheng et al., CVPR 2020, arXiv 2005.02551; MIT, pretrained model, `pip install segmentation-refinement`, class-agnostic). Per-region refinement at the original resolution, so it doubles as an FM10 tool: refine the nearest-upsampled group map against the full-res photo instead of the luminance guided filter.
- **reference-only: SegFix** (Yuan et al., ECCV 2020; `openseg-group/openseg.pytorch`, MIT). Model-agnostic with respect to the segmenter, but the boundary-to-interior offset predictor is a trained network (the paper trains it on the target datasets, Cityscapes and ADE20K); no product-photo checkpoint exists, so CascadePSP/SAMRefiner rank above it.
- **reference-only: SegRefiner** (NeurIPS 2023, Apache-2.0; discrete-diffusion refinement, slow), **Convolutional Oriented Boundaries / MCG** (ECCV 2016), **Semantic-SAM** (2023, no licence file), **SAMA** (AAAI 2026; unified segmentation + matting, code not stated on the abstract page).
- **experiment: alpha matting for mixed pixels — ViTMatte** (Yao et al. 2023, arXiv 2305.15272; `hustvl/ViTMatte`, MIT). Trimap = dilated label boundary; the alpha replaces the hard label indicator that `_coverage` snaps today. **Matte Anything** (2023; licence not checked) is the SAM-driven trimap variant. **MatAnyone** (CVPR 2025) is S-Lab License 1.0, non-commercial, so it stays in scratch. **Semantic Soft Segmentation** (Aksoy et al., SIGGRAPH 2018) is MATLAB plus a TensorFlow feature network, "academic use only" (LICENSE checked), minutes per image: reference-only. Benchmark: the perceptually motivated matting benchmark (Rhemann et al., CVPR 2009) for alpha error on a small hand-made trimap set.
- **reference-only: The Fast Bilateral Solver** (Barron & Poole, ECCV 2016). `opencv-python-headless` has no `ximgproc`; the numpy port is slow at 1536 px; the torch guided filters in `recolor/filters.py` are the fast edge-aware option available here.
- **experiment (FM2 half of the card): Grounding DINO + Grounded-SAM-2** (Liu et al., ECCV 2024, arXiv 2303.05499; Apache-2.0; COCO zero-shot 48.1 AP for the Swin-T model, 172 M parameters, 8.37 FPS on an unstated GPU, Table 20). Text phrases ("mirror . tyre . windscreen . headlight .") → boxes → SAM 2 box prompts on the already-loaded predictor → new regions with `source='detected'` for the parts SAM's point grid missed. Fit-check corrections that must be honoured: run in `pipeline._stage_regions` before `build_regions` and pass detections in as a new argument (the contract in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.2 changes); intersect the box-prompted mask, not the box, with regions (≥ 60 % of a region inside the mask); override the HF processor's default 800/1333 resize (it would downscale the 1536 px work image); add the model to the idle-unload list in `pipeline.py` and to `/api/health` in `server/app.py`. Environment: `transformers` 5.17.0 resolves for this venv without a CUDA build (its deformable attention is pure torch); `openai`, `faiss`, `pydensecrf` are absent.
- **reference-only: part segmentation** (VLPart ICCV 2023, MIT, archived; PartGLEE ECCV 2024; PartImageNet 2021) and **text detection** (DBNet AAAI 2020; CRAFT CVPR 2019, MIT): semantic granularity and text boxes, not edge accuracy; the decal case is covered by `decal_split.py`.

### FM3 Intrinsic decomposition

- **current: Careaga & Aksoy, Colorful Diffuse Intrinsic Image Decomposition in the Wild** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 / TOG, arXiv 2409.13690; code `compphoto/Intrinsic`, academic-use-only licence). Sharp albedo that carries highlights and paint bounce in the shading (numbers in section 2).
- **experiment → adopt candidate: Marigold-IID shading colour graft (`mg_hyb`)** (Marigold, Ke et al. 2025, arXiv 2505.09358, journal extension of CVPR 2024; weights `prs-eth/marigold-iid-lighting-v1-1`, CreativeML Open RAIL++-M; diffusers pipeline Apache-2.0). Marigold's shading is nearly neutral (Ducati shading chroma 10.3→4.4 median, 33.6→6.5 p90). Grafting only its chromaticity onto Careaga's shading gives Ducati navy hue drift 0.4°/3.4° (best of every variant), BMW 2.1°/20.9°, everything else unchanged. Cost: 0.90 s per image at native 1536 px, 6.0 GB allocated, 2.6 GB checkpoint, `diffusers` + `transformers` in a venv (the experiment used `scratch/envs/intrinsic`, which left torch/numpy untouched).
- **reject for now: full Marigold-IID swap.** Cleaner layers (albedo R² 0.01 on the Ducati) but blurry part boundaries from the VAE decoder (jaggy 0.87 even after an edge snap), 2-3× more paint energy parked in a dense paint-coloured residual (0.32-0.36 vs 0.14-0.19) that the engine's split discards on dark targets (black-tank structure r 0.68→0.46), and a per-material albedo scale that no global calibration fixes (navy lands 8-10 L units lighter on the BMW). Every number: intrinsic README result tables.
- **reject as a renderer, experiment as a cue: RGB↔X** (Zeng et al., SIGGRAPH 2024, arXiv 2405.00666; code `zheng95z/rgbx`, Adobe Research licence; weights on Hugging Face, 13.8 GB for the three models). X→RGB cannot reproduce the photograph (5.1-6.8 dB far from the Ducati; the VAE alone caps at 25-28 dB) and deletes the reflections it is asked to recolour (97-99 % of 2720 reflection pixels come back neutral). RGB→X's roughness/metallic maps are the only per-pixel material signal already measured on our photos (trial 1: "plausible") and feed FM4/FM5.
- **reference-only: Intrinsic Image Diffusion** (Kocsis et al., CVPR 2024; CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0; indoor, multi-hypothesis albedo/roughness/metallic), **DiffusionRenderer** (NVIDIA, CVPR 2025; NVIDIA licence; video G-buffers), **S-Aware** (Jin et al., AAAI 2023; reflectance layer with shadow/specular awareness; the repository ships the classical formula only, no weights): moved here from colour science and demoted.
- **reference-only: illuminant estimation** (Convolutional Color Constancy ICCV 2015 and FFCC CVPR 2017, `google/ffcc` Apache-2.0, archived; C5 ICCV 2021, Apache-2.0; FC4 CVPR 2017, MIT; Grey-Edge TIP 2007; AID CVPR 2024; CCMNet 2025). These are trained and evaluated on camera-raw / log-chroma data; our inputs are already white-balanced JPEGs, and the engine already has an illuminant estimate in `Renderer._light_reference`. The only cheap check worth doing is Grey-Edge (no weights) against `_light_reference` on the six jobs. **Deep spectral reflectance and illuminant estimation from self-interreflections** (JOSA A 2019) assumes a controlled two-surface geometry with spectral measurements: not applicable to a single sRGB photo, demoted from the previous draft's priority list.
- **reference-only: SingleHDR** (Liu et al., CVPR 2020; code and weights, licence unstated): reconstructs clipped highlights by inverting the camera pipeline; relevant to FM5's unknown highlight colour, unmeasured.

### FM4 Material awareness

- **reference-only (paper), experiment (idea): GaussianProperty** (Xu et al. 2024, arXiv 2412.11258; `EnVision-Research/Gaussian-Property`, no licence file). Segments with SAM, asks a cloud LMM (gpt-4o-mini or Qwen-VL-Max in the shipped code) for a material per region, votes across views. Fact-check correction: Table 2 reads voting-only 22.17 % mIoU, global-to-local-only 51.28 %, both 55.83 %, so the single-view figure is 51.28 %, not 22.17 % as the card first said. Fit-check (refuted, major): labelling per ColorGroup would label mixed groups (section 2), every `_finalize`/`regroup` would drop a group-level field, a new stage cannot run without extending `types.STAGES` and three UI files, cloud calls inside `gpu_lock` would block exports, and a binary "confidence" makes the threshold vacuous. What survives: per-region labelling by a local model, used to split mixed neutral groups and to suggest "unmapped", with `grouping.py` carrying the field through `_carry_all`/`_group_from`/`_finalize` and `regroup` re-deriving it from regions.
- **reject: Segment Any Vehicle** (Wang et al., IEEE TCSVT 2026, arXiv 2508.04260; `Event-AHU/SAV`, MIT; VehicleSeg10K 11,665 images, 13 structural classes, 81.23 mIoU, 313 M parameters). No released weights; a pinned `pytorch-cuda=12.1` / mmcv stack that cannot run on an sm_120 RTX 5090 (verified: no mmcv wheel for Python 3.13, sdist build fails); six car body types only; no material classes (its single "Wheel" fuses rim and tyre). The licence is confirmed MIT; the dataset's terms are unstated and the retrieval stream needs training images at inference.
- **experiment (advisory only): GDNet / GDNet-B glass detection** (Mei et al., CVPR 2020; TPAMI extension arXiv 2209.04639; `Mhaiyang/CVPR2020_GDNet`, BSD-3-style licence with non-canonical wording; CVPR-2020 weights confirmed, the TPAMI "-B" weights not). GDD IoU 87.63 (CVPR) / 87.83 (B); 0.034 s at 416² on a GTX 1080Ti. Fit-check (refuted, major) changes the consumption model: do not auto-lock. A headlight over chrome already sits in a neutral group; where glass clusters with the paint it is because the paint shows through it, and on a repaint the right default is the new paint behind the screen. So: tag `source='glass'` regions, keep them out of albedo clustering, show a badge and a "suggest unmapped" default that is off when the glass albedo is within ΔE 10 of a mapped paint group. Port is ~0.5 day (`torch.load(weights_only=False)`, `F.interpolate`, skip the inference-only `dss_crf`), aspect-preserving 416 px input plus crops for parts that are ~10 px after resizing. Gate adoption on false positives over the 29 glass-free samples of the 35 in `samples/MANIFEST.json`.
- **experiment (scripts-only): Dense Material Segmentation** (Upchurch & Niu, ECCV 2022, arXiv 2207.10614; `apple/ml-dms-dataset`: 46-class model, 170 MB, Apple licence; data CC-BY-NC 4.0). One multi-class pass covering metal, glass and more (the README does not enumerate the 46 classes; rubber/paint/chrome coverage unverified). Per-region majority vote → `material_hint`. The licence keeps it in `scripts/`, not the MIT package.
- **reference-only: MatSeg, zero-shot material-state segmentation** (Eppel et al. 2024, arXiv 2403.03309; code, model and 300 k textures released; named in the SAV fit-check, not deep-read).
- **experiment: zero-shot region naming** — CLIPSeg (Lüddecke & Ecker, CVPR 2022; MIT code, weights excluded from the licence), Grounding DINO (above), RAM/RAM++ (2023, Apache-2.0) as a tagger. The cheapest variant is a SigLIP/CLIP classification of each region's masked crop against a fixed vocabulary (paint, chrome, brushed metal, glass, clear plastic, rubber, carbon fibre, fabric/leather, other); no second segmentation pass, no box-to-region ambiguity.
- **reference-only: material editing generators** — ZeST (ECCV 2024, MIT), Alchemist (CVPR 2024, no code), In-the-wild Material Appearance Editing (EG 2023, no code), Material Anything (CVPR 2025, MIT, 3D meshes), DualMat (2025, no code). They change material, not colour, through diffusion re-rendering, which breaks the identity contract (`render({})` bit-exact).
- **reference-only: Photo-realistic Rendering of Metallic Car Paint from Image-Based Measurements** (CGF 2008). Needs measured BRDF data and offers no way to detect or edit flake/pearl paint in a photo; metallic paint (FM4/FM5) remains uncovered by any surfaced card and is the big bet in section 4.

### FM5 Highlights and speculars

- **experiment (model-free first): local normalisation of `spec_weight`** and a classical specular-free cue (min-channel specular-free image, as the JSHDR fit-check recommends, in torch, no weights). Replace the single global 0.99 quantile by a per-group or box-filtered local quantile so a broad softbox reflection and a pin glint are not judged by one threshold. Metric: the black-target "structure r" from the intrinsic README (Pearson r between the photo's L and the black render's L inside the paint; Ducati 0.68 today) plus the harness.
- **reference-only: JSHDR** (Fu et al., CVPR 2021; 16 k-quadruple SHIQ dataset; PSNR 34.13 on SHIQ, Table 2). No public code (the SHIQ, JSHDR and SHDNet repos return 404); the card's year field was wrong (2021, not 2026). Fit-check: a highlight mask fed into `_adjusted_residual` can only change the amplitude of the neutral floor, never rotate hue, so the FM5 hue-drift defect is untouched; its formation model assumes colourless highlights. **TSHRNet** (Fu et al., ICCV 2023, arXiv 2309.06302; `fu123456/TSHRNet`, no licence, weights on Google Drive, Python 3.9 / torch 1.13 / CUDA 11.6) is the live successor and outputs a specular-free image, not a mask; scratch-only experiment if the model-free cue fails.
- **adopt (via FM6): OKLab `toe`** restores the dark-repaint balance that a pure OK-L port crushes (BMW black interior L median 15.2 shipping vs 6.3 pure OK vs 16.7 toe).
- **big bet: dielectric vs metallic speculars** need a per-pixel metallic/roughness estimate (RGB→X, Intrinsic Image Diffusion, DiffusionRenderer): tint the surviving glint by the paint where metallic is high, keep it white where it is low. No measured gain yet.

### FM6 Colour math

- **adopt: Oklab** (Ottosson 2020; public-domain reference code; also `colour.XYZ_to_Oklab` in colour-science, BSD-3). The `oklab_toe` variant keeps the architecture (coverage, residual split, retint, pivot) and swaps the space: BMW navy 4.8°/23.0° → 2.1°/7.1°, BMW pastel 3.1°/8.3° → 2.2°/7.9°, Ducati navy 0.7°/9.4° → 0.4°/21.0° (the −21° bin holds 1.1 % of the paint: tank highlights and decal edges), Ducati pastel 1.2°/2.9° → 2.7°/6.3° (a 2-4° cyan bias from the bounce retint). Harness on all six jobs: 11484→11473 halo, 277→282 bleed, worst jaggy 1.404→1.400, identity 0. Render time parity (5.0 / 5.7 ms preview vs 4.8 / 5.5). 58/59 engine tests pass; the failing one is a chroma bound of 9.0 met at 9.16. The `toe_leak` option (retint on the measured leak direction) passes 59/59 and is best on the BMW (navy 1.9°/6.8°, pastel 1.4°/4.6°) but puts +7..+9° purple into the Ducati pastel shadows; keep it off until the rotation origin is handled per pixel.
- **experiment: CSS Color Module Level 4 gamut mapping** (W3C 2022; colorjs.io reference, MIT). The engine already searches chroma at fixed L and hue; the change is OKLCh plus the ΔE-OK JND stopping rule, and, for pastel targets, a cusp-aware mapping (chroma relative to the gamut boundary at the *target's* lightness), which the fixed-L `toe_gamut` trial showed is needed (34-37 % of a navy render pushed onto the boundary otherwise).
- **reference-only: CAM16 / CAT16 / CAM16-UCS** (Li et al. 2017; colour-science BSD-3), **Jzazbz** (2017), **IPT** (1998), **ProLab** (2020), **hue-linearized CIELAB gamut mapping** (1998): alternative spaces; none has a measured advantage over OKLab for this engine. **Oklch+** (Uchida, arXiv 2606.05255, June 2026): a colour-difference metric close to CIEDE2000 with fewer parameters; a candidate replacement for `imageio.delta_e` in grouping, reference-only until the `blend` rule has landed.

### FM7 Palette

- **experiment: harmony re-ranking with Color Compatibility From Large Datasets** (O'Donovan et al., SIGGRAPH 2011; data and code on the project page). Score the k-means candidates (`extract_palette` produces n + 4 centroids) and the theme pads by the learned compatibility regressor; keep the top n. Offline, no model download beyond the paper's coefficients.
- **experiment: Text2Colors** (Bahng et al., ECCV 2018, arXiv 1804.04128; MIT). A text→palette GAN trained on the PAT dataset; local, no key; quality on prompts like "stealth matte" unverified.
- **experiment (opt-in only): ColorGPT** (ICDAR 2025, arXiv 2508.08987, no code) and LLM colour-word association (2024, arXiv 2411.02116). The palette module is built to run without keys and never fail offline (`generate_palette` falls back to theme/parsed/default). An LLM suggester must therefore be an explicit opt-in backend: the `anthropic` SDK is already in the venv (`openai` is not), or a small local instruct model; the result merges through `_append_distinct` like image colours.
- **reference-only: Color Harmonization** (Cohen-Or et al., SIGGRAPH 2006; hue templates), **Probabilistic Color-by-Numbers** (Lin et al., SIGGRAPH 2013), **Aesthetic Rating and Color Suggestion for Color Palettes** (PG 2016), **Colormind** (web), **Semantic Context Matters** (arXiv 2607.17221, July 2026: colour names occupy different regions per domain, cosmetics vs crayons vs automobiles, a hint that automotive colour vocabularies deserve their own table in `colornames`).
- **moved out of palette generation: text-to-image colour control** — GenColor (CHI 2025), Palette2Image (ACM MM 2025), ColorPeel (ECCV 2024). They condition an image generator on colours; none produces a palette from a prompt without a generation step. reference-only.

### FM8 Mapping

- **experiment: part-aware costs from the material/part tags of FM4** — a hard set (glass, tyre, rubber, plate) defaults to unmapped, chrome/metal gets a cost bonus against body colours, wheels vs body are named. This is a policy patch, not aesthetic reasoning (Grounding DINO card, honest expected gain).
- **experiment: harmony term** from the O'Donovan regressor over the whole assignment (score each Hungarian solution's induced palette, pick the best of the top-k assignments).
- **reference-only: Probabilistic Color-by-Numbers** is the one surfaced paper that models "which colour goes where" from spatial adjacency (a factor graph over neighbouring regions); it needs training data of coloured patterns we do not have.

### FM9 Evaluation

- **adopt (scripts only): LPIPS** (Zhang et al., CVPR 2018; BSD-2-Clause), **DISTS** (Ding et al., TPAMI 2022; MIT), **FLIP** (Andersson et al., HPG 2020; BSD-3-Clause) as full-reference metrics once a reference exists (section 6). **IQA-PyTorch** (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0) may be used in `scripts/` on this machine but cannot ship in the MIT package.
- **experiment: no-reference and judge models** — Q-Align (2023), ImageReward (NeurIPS 2023, Apache-2.0), VIEScore (ACL 2024), PICABench (2025; physical-realism editing benchmark, code released), GEditBench v2 / PVC-Judge (arXiv 2603.28547, March 2026; open pairwise visual-consistency judge). Use for ranking candidates, never as the acceptance bar.
- **reference-only: PieAPP** (CVPR 2018; pairwise-preference protocol worth copying for a user study), **Learning a Deep Color Difference Metric for Photographic Images** (CVPR 2023).
- **datasets:** Hypersim (ICCV 2021; Apple licence), OpenRooms (CVPR 2021; MIT), Stanford-ORB (NeurIPS 2023 D&B; no licence file), ColourfulSet (arXiv 2603.18466, March 2026: paired images with continuous colour variations; the paper's project URL is not on the abstract page), the alpha-matting benchmark (CVPR 2009) for FM2 alphas.

### FM10 Full-resolution export

- **experiment: colour-guided upsampling of layers and labels** — the guided filter is the local-affine model that **Bilateral Guided Upsampling** (Chen, Adams, Wadhwa, Hasinoff, SIGGRAPH Asia 2016; `google/bgu`, Apache-2.0, MATLAB/Halide/GLSL, archived) generalises to a bilateral grid, and **Joint Bilateral Upsampling** (Kopf, Cohen, Lischinski, Uyttendaele, SIGGRAPH 2007; PDF only) is the range-weighted variant; the practical torch path is `guided_filter_color` at full res for albedo and shading, with the residual recomputed as today.
- **experiment: CascadePSP at export resolution** for the group map (above), and **tiled full-res Careaga inference** above 12 MP (engineering, not a paper: the model is 1.5 GB/MP by the pipeline's own estimate, so 2×2 tiles with a 64 px overlap fit any 6000 px upload in 32 GB).

### FM11 Global consistency

- **adopt (gated): in-engine hue rotation of out-of-group old-paint pixels** (rerender README trial 5; numbers in section 1). Half (a) of FM11. The soft gate is the whole design: chroma > 20, hue within 30° of the source paint, distance falloff from the repainted groups, exclusion of locked groups, and a hue margin that protects the gold wheels (the feathered control let partial rotation of gold pass through red).
- **experiment: neighbour bounce** — half (b): apply the `_retint_shading` decomposition to pixels of adjacent unpainted groups within a distance falloff of the repainted boundary. Unmeasured; metric: hue drift of the neighbour band vs the untouched far region.
- **reject: Intrinsic Image Harmonization** (Guo et al., CVPR 2021; `zhenglab/IntrinsicHarmony`, MIT; iHarmony4 PSNR 35.90 in the paper, 36.81 in the README). Fit-check fatal: inference is `comp·(1−mask) + harmonized·mask`, so with a paint-only mask the reflection pixels (≥ 20 px outside the paint by the metric's definition) cannot change; survival stays 1.00 by construction, and a 256² round trip would only damage the paint interior. Effort to learn this: zero.
- **reject: PCT-Net** (Guerreiro et al., CVPR 2023; MPL-2.0; full-res iHarmony4 fMSE 238.27 / PSNR 39.28 for the ViT variant vs the re-evaluated DCCF row 302.89 / 25.34 / 37.60 dB, fact-check corrected). Needs a binary mask of what to correct; scattered reflections have none; a ring mask straddling both sides constructs the rim-of-old-paint bug `_coverage` removed. Its only honest placement would be after `render_once` on uint8 sRGB, gated on an empty mapping.
- **reference-only: Harmonizer** (ECCV 2022; white-box filter regression), **Reflecting Reality / MirrorFusion** (3DV 2025; diffusion inpainting of mirror reflections, needs an object mask and synthetic training), **Neural Gaffer** (2024, MIT) and **IC-Light** (Apache-2.0) (relighting), **mirror and glass detectors** MirrorNet (ICCV 2019), PMD (CVPR 2020, no code), HetNet (AAAI 2023), Mirror3D (CVPR 2021), SAM2 shadow/mirror (2024), EBLNet (ICCV 2021): a reflective-surface mask would tell the FM11 rotation where a red pixel is a reflection rather than a red part; unmeasured, and the rerender README notes the Ducati's reflections are in chrome, brake calliper and floor, not mirrors.
- **reference-only: diffusion editors as an optional post-pass** — Qwen-Image-Edit (2025, Apache-2.0; 20 B parameters, needs quantisation to fit 32 GB), HiDream-E1 (2025, MIT), FLUX.1 Kontext dev (2025; non-commercial licence, as is ICEdit which builds on it), Step1X-Edit (Apache-2.0), InstructPix2Pix, SDEdit, ColorEdit (no code), the 2025 survey. They break the pipeline's contract that only albedo is edited and identity reproduces the photo; the one scoping that survives the rerender experiment is a fidelity-masked blend, which "passes the metric, fails the eye" (survival 0.012, PSNR 35.6 dB, bright re-lit speckles pasted into the dark photo).

## 5. Experiment plan

Ordered; each names inputs, metric and the decision rule. All run from `scratch/experiments/<name>/` with a `run_metrics.py` that recomputes every number, as the four existing experiments do.

- **E1 Grouping distance** (`grouping/`, exists). Inputs: six job bundles. Metric: paint-locked main share, guard pairs, harness. Decision: the Numbers table already passes Q1; implement in `grouping.py` and re-run `run_metrics.py` against the package (not the scratch copy) before merging.
- **E2 Colour-guided label snap** (new `refine/`). Inputs: `labels.npy` of the six jobs, `work.png`. Variants: luminance r2 (today), colour r2, colour r3. Metric: harness (halo/bleed/jaggy), plus boundary offset against 20 hand-marked edges. Decision: adopt the variant with the lowest halo at bleed ≤ 277 and jaggy ≤ 1.40; else keep luminance.
- **E3 OKLab** (`oklab/`, exists). Decision rule already met (section 4, Q2); port and re-run `run_metrics.py` against the package.
- **E4 FM11 gate** (`rerender/` control → new `reflections/`). Inputs: navy renders of Ducati and BMW (after E1). Sweep: chroma cut 15/20/25, hue window 20/30/40°, falloff 20/40/80 px, gold-margin 15/20°. Metric: reflection survival, PSNR outside paint vs engine render, gold-group ΔE. Decision: adopt the smallest gate reaching survival ≤ 0.05 with gold ΔE < 2.
- **E5 Marigold shading colour on six jobs** (`intrinsic/`, extend). Inputs: the four remaining jobs' `work.png` through `run_marigold.py` (native 1536 px, 4 steps, seed 0). Metric: navy hue drift mean/worst per job, harness, structure r on black. Decision: adopt if M1 passes; else keep the hybrid as an opt-in `intrinsic="careaga+mg"` option.
- **E6 Material cue from stored layers** (new `material/`). Inputs: per-region positive-residual energy fraction, shading chroma, albedo chroma from `albedo.npy`/`shading.npy`/`residual.npy`/`labels.npy`; hand-labelled material per region on the six jobs plus `motorcycle_2`, `car_red_sports_2`, `car_classic_1/2`. Metric: per-region precision/recall for tyre, glass, chrome vs black paint. Decision: if precision ≥ 0.8 at recall ≥ 0.6 for any class, ship that class as a badge; otherwise go to E7.
- **E7 Zero-shot region classifier** (same directory). Variants: SigLIP/CLIP on masked crops with the nine-word vocabulary; Grounding DINO → SAM 2 masks (with the processor size override); GDNet glass tag as an extra channel. Metric: as E6 plus false-positive area on the 29 glass-free samples. - **E8 Boundary bake-off** (new `boundary/`). Inputs: `motorcycle_1`, `car_red_sports_1`, `gundam_sazabi_verka_a`, `sprue_runner_1`, `street_complex_1` with 20-40 hand-labelled thin-part masks each. - **E9 Full-res export** (new `fullres/`). Inputs: three uploads > 12 MP (crop/tile the 6000 px originals if none exist) and three ≤ 12 MP. Variants: today's path; colour-guided upsampling; CascadePSP labels; tiled Careaga. Metric: M4's three numbers. Decision: adopt the variant passing M4 with export time ≤ 2× today's.
- **E10 Specular weight** (new `specular/`). Variants: global quantile (today), per-group quantile, box-filtered local quantile, min-channel specular-free cue. Metric: black-target structure r, harness, visual sheet of the Ducati tank. Decision: adopt if r improves by ≥ 0.05 on the Ducati without a harness regression.
- **E11 Ground truth build** (section 6). Decision: only after E11 do LPIPS/DISTS/FLIP numbers enter any adoption rule.
- **E12 Palette harmony and opt-in LLM** (new `palette/`). Inputs: 30 prompts with hand-picked "good" palettes. Metric: rank of the hand-picked palette among candidates; user preference on 30 pairs. Decision: adopt the re-ranker if it improves rank on ≥ 20/30 prompts; the LLM path ships only as opt-in with the offline fallback intact.
- **E13 Metallic cue (big bet)** (`rerender/` rgb→x channels exist for both bikes). Inputs: RGB→X metallic/roughness maps per region. Metric: does per-region median metallic separate chrome/gold from paint on the hand-labelled set (E6)? Decision: if yes, design the two-mode specular model and test it on E11's synthetic pairs.

## 6. New evaluation

**Ground truth.** The only way to get pixel-exact recoloured pairs is to re-render scenes whose albedo we control. Three surfaced sources: Stanford-ORB (real objects with measured captures and an inverse-rendering benchmark; no licence file, check before use), OpenRooms (MIT; end-to-end indoor scene assets) and Hypersim (Apple licence; photorealistic indoor renders with per-pixel intrinsics). Plan: pick 20-30 objects with painted or dyed surfaces, render the scene, change only the object's albedo (five targets spanning dark/light/saturated/pastel), re-render, and store `(photo, target hex, recoloured photo, part mask, alpha at the boundary)`. This gives references for full-reference metrics, exact boundary alphas for FM2, exact reflections for FM11 and exact highlight behaviour for FM5. Complement with the real paired set ColourfulSet (inspect first: the paper describes continuous colour variations, provenance of the pairs to be checked) and with unpaired real photos of the same product in several factory colours (usable only for distribution checks: does the hue/chroma distribution of a repainted panel match a real panel of that colour?).

**Metrics to add.** Full-reference on the synthetic set: PSNR, LPIPS (BSD-2), DISTS (MIT), FLIP (BSD-3), and a ΔE-OK error map split by region (paint interior, boundary band, reflections, highlights). Boundary: alpha error (SAD/MSE/gradient, the matting-benchmark protocol) between `_coverage` and the rendered alpha, and the boundary-offset test of M4. FM4: correct-lock and wrong-lock rates against hand labels. FM11: reflection survival stays, plus reflection hue error against the synthetic reference. No-reference (scripts only, ranking not acceptance): ImageReward, Q-Align, PVC-Judge/PICABench-style physical-consistency judges. Human: a PieAPP-style pairwise preference study, 20 photos × 3 targets, shipping vs candidate, before any "big bet" is merged. Licensing rule: PolyForm-NC (IQA-PyTorch) and any non-commercial weights stay in `scripts/` and `scratch/`, never in `recolor/`.
